Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-22. This explains what we hold about you and why.

What we collect

  • Your username — shown publicly on leaderboards. That is its purpose.
  • Your name — only if you give one. Kept for our own records, never shown to anyone else and never put on a leaderboard.
  • Your email — only if you give one. Used to help you recover your account, and for occasional updates if you asked for them.
  • Your birth year — only if you give it, and only the year, never the month or day. Used solely in aggregate, alongside everyone else's, to understand the age range of people who play — never shown to anyone, never used to single you out, and never collected for anyone below the age this game requires.
  • Your password — stored only as a scrypt hash. We cannot read it.

Deleting your account removes everything below too — your recorded shots, puzzle answers, and duel history, not just your ability to log in. If you appear on a leaderboard, deleting your account removes you from it.

  • Your play — scores, camera settings at the moment of each shot, puzzle answers, XP and streaks. This is what makes the leaderboards and your own statistics work, and what tells us which rounds and puzzles are too hard or too easy. It is never shared with advertisers or used to target ads, and it is never sold to anyone.
  • Basic technical data — the address your requests come from, used for rate limiting and to spot abuse. It is never used to work out where you are.
  • How long you play, and when — the start and end of each visit, so we can see whether people are staying and enjoying it. The clock we record it against is your device's own, so that "people play in the evening" means their evening.
  • Your device's time zone, language and type — the region your device is set to (for example Europe/London), the language it is set to, and whether it is a phone, tablet or computer. The time zone is a setting on your device, not a measurement of where you are: it is accurate to roughly the country and no closer, and everyone from Maine to Miami reports the same one. We use it to tell which countries the game has reached, whether it is worth translating, and whether it runs badly on phones. We do not use GPS, we do not ask your browser for your position, and we do not look your address up in any geolocation service.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, no tracking across other sites, no precise or device location, no contacts, no photos from your camera roll, and no card details. Nothing in the game is for sale today.

Who else sees it

  • Everyone: your username, your scores on public boards, and any badge you wear.
  • Our hosting provider: they store the database on our behalf.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell data.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. Delete your account and it goes.

Your rights

You can download everything we hold about you, and delete your account, from the You tab in the game — no email needed. If you can no longer get into the game, visit shutterblip.com/delete-account or write to Daniel@MoonVisionMedia.com. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to object, to restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

This game is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to Daniel@MoonVisionMedia.com and we will remove it.
